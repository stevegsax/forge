## Problem

pbook has no evals at all, while extraction noise runs ~30% unvalidated
per agent-memory research — extraction-prompt changes, retrieval-ranking
changes, and model-pin moves are currently unverifiable. The merged plan's
quality constants and gates are explicitly provisional and must be set
from a measured baseline rather than a priori, which requires committed
golden suites for extraction (Suite A) and retrieval (Suite C); judge
calibration (Suite B) is [T6.2](T6.2-judge-calibration.md).

## Background and Detailed Explanation

T6.6 builds the yardstick that makes every other Phase 6 change
verifiable — pbook currently has no evals at all.

**The problem.** Around a third of extracted knowledge is noise, yet
nothing measures it. That means three kinds of change are currently
flying blind: edits to the extraction prompt, changes to retrieval
ranking, and moves of the model pins — each could silently make quality
worse with no signal. Compounding this, the merged plan's quality
constants and gates — thresholds sprinkled through the earlier Phase 6
tasks and marked "provisional" — were set a priori, from intuition
rather than measurement. There is no baseline to say what "good" even
is.

**The fix.** Commit two golden eval suites (the third, judge
calibration, is T6.2's Suite B). Suite A grades extraction — growing
from 30 toward 100 cases, including 10 "must-extract-zero" negatives
gated at 100% (inputs the extractor must produce *nothing* from) and
positives held to ≥80%, and it is grown over time by mining the
validator's own rejections. Suite C grades retrieval against a frozen
~80-entry corpus whose embeddings are pre-computed by a PEP-723 helper
script and committed, so the suite makes zero API calls at test time —
about 50 labeled queries across modes. Both live under `tests/evals/`,
marked `eval` and excluded from default runs, behind a `make evals`
target.

**The discipline that matters.** Gates are set from a *measured
baseline run minus a tolerance*, not from a priori numbers — this is
where the provisional constants scattered through the phase get
replaced with real ones. And model pins move only on green
paired-delta runs: a pin change must be shown not to regress the suites
before it lands.

**How you'll know it's done:** `make evals` is green and the baseline
numbers are recorded in the commit that introduces the suites.

## Scope

- `tests/evals/` marked `eval`, excluded from default runs; `make evals`.
- Suite A extraction goldens (30→100 cases; 10 must-extract-zero
  negatives gated 100%, positives ≥80%; grown by mining validator
  rejections).
- Suite C retrieval goldens (~80-entry frozen corpus, embeddings
  pre-computed by a PEP-723 helper and committed — zero API calls at test
  time; ~50 labeled queries across modes).
- **Gates set from a measured baseline run minus tolerance** (not a
  priori); model pins move only on green paired-delta runs.
